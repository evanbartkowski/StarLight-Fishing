Starlight Fishing – Free Online Browser Fishing Adventure Game

Welcome to Starlight Fishing (starlightfishing.com), the premier free online browser-based fishing adventure game. Set sail across seven enchanted ocean seas, cast your hook into shimmering celestial depths, and reel in legendary ocean titans, mythical sea creatures, and ancient sunken relics.

Explore Seven Enchanted Oceanic Realms

  • Sunlit Shoals: Calm harbor waters teeming with colorful reef fish, playful dolphins, and sunny starting waters.
  • Twilight Kelp Forest: Towering kelp groves concealing elusive species, electric eels, and sunken galleons.
  • Astral Shimmerfall: Shimmering celestial waters adorned with falling starlight, cosmic nebulae, and celestial rays.
  • Sunken Atlantis: Ancient submerged marble ruins guarded by graceful mermaids, colossal krakens, and mythological sea serpents.
  • Whispering Aether Sea: A dreamlike sea beneath floating cloud palaces where mythical underwater unicorns and sky manta cruise.
  • Molten Caldera Trench: Intense volcanic vents and magma currents inhabited by the feared Molten Megalodon.
  • Eldritch Chrono Void: The mysterious outer boundary of time and space, where giant cosmic whales and temporal anomalies glide through the dark.

Core Features

  • Hundreds of exotic fish species, crowns, and colossal ocean titans to discover and log in your Fish Journal.
  • In-depth tackle progression: upgrade fishing rods, line tension, descent speed, sonar scanners, and automated drones.
  • Sunken treasures: dig up ancient chests, golden relics, valuable pearls, and mysterious artifacts.
  • Personal Aquarium: transfer your favorite catches into your custom aquarium tank to feed them and earn passive gold.
  • Global Player Community: live chat, world leaderboards, and social sharing.
  • Instant Access: play immediately as a guest or create a cloud account to keep your captain profile safe across devices.

How to Play

Aim your rod with your mouse or finger, click or tap to cast into the water, and navigate your hook through schools of fish while dodging sharp obstacles. When you latch onto a creature, reel it back to the surface to sell for coins or keep in your aquarium!
